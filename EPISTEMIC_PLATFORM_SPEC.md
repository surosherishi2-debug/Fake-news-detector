## 1. The Transmutation: Why "Fake News Detection" Alone Is Obsolete

In academic capstone reviews and industrial deployments, a simple "Fake News Detector" faces immediate fatal critiques:
1. *"It's just a binary text classifier."* (Evaluators dismiss it as an introductory NLP homework project).
2. *"It's purely reactive."* (A user has to already suspect a piece of news, copy-paste it into a box, and wait).
3. *"It ignores the creator side."* (Journalists and students need tools to verify their *own* reporting before publication).
4. *"It cannot tell AI hallucinations from human lies."* (Generative AI has flooded the web with synthetically hallucinated articles that are neither purely true nor malicious human disinformation).

## 2. Expanded Data Architecture & Persistent Knowledge Base

To eliminate the appearance of a hardcoded toy app, the system is backed by an **Embedded Epistemic Knowledge Database (SQLite + Vector Store)** pre-populated with:
- **Real Benchmark Records:** Curated splits from `PuneFakeNews-2024` (bilingual English/Hindi), `FakeNewsNet` (PolitiFact + GossipCop), and `LIAR`.
- **Live RSS Multi-Source Ingestion:** Automated collectors monitoring news wires (Reuters, PIB, BBC, Nature, The Hindu, BoomLive) and satire feeds (The Onion).
- **Persistent Historical Entity Store:** Mapping 500+ global entities, medical terms, government agencies, and political actors to their official verification records.
